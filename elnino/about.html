How it was built

  • Impact records from OCHA, IFRC, Kenya Red Cross, WFP, USAID/OFDA, FAO and Nation Africa (1997–98, 2009–10, 2015–16).
  • Boundaries from geoBoundaries — Kenya ADM1 (all 47 counties).
  • 2026 index = 0.40 × historical recurrence + 0.35 × ICPAC/KMD forecast signal + 0.25 × structural exposure.
  • Atlantic module from NOAA / NHC HURDAT2 counts against the historical ENSO phase.

Method & interpretation

Tier = documented impact level in the two events (severe / high / moderate / watch / minimal). "Benefit" flags counties where the extra rainfall was reported as largely positive for crops or livestock. "Watch" = national flooding was reported but county-specific detail is limited. Numbers are the ranges quoted by the cited agencies, not exact census figures.

2026 model. Risk (0-100) = 0.40 x historical recurrence + 0.35 x ICPAC/KMD 2026 forecast signal + 0.25 x structural exposure. It is a transparent planning index, not a physics-based hydrological forecast; forecast skill declines for smaller areas and beyond a season.
